# Email marketing plan

## Recommendation: Kit

Kit is the best starting fit for an author-led newsletter. Its current Newsletter plan supports up to 10,000 subscribers, unlimited forms and landing pages, unlimited email broadcasts, one basic automation, and one email sequence. The free plan requires one Kit-managed creator recommendation slot.

MailerLite is the strongest alternative when a conventional WordPress plugin and a simpler form workflow matter more than the free subscriber allowance. Its current free plan is limited to 250 subscribers and 2,500 monthly emails. Brevo is a useful alternative when marketing mail and transactional site email need to share one provider.

## Integration approach

1. Create the email-provider account in Madelyn's name and verify her sending address and domain.
2. Add the legally required sender address, privacy language, consent text, and unsubscribe behavior.
3. Create a dedicated website signup form with double opt-in enabled.
4. Create a short welcome sequence and tag subscribers by source as `website`.
5. Paste the provider's hosted form URL into **Appearance → Customize → Contact & newsletter → Newsletter signup URL**.
6. After the account is approved, replace the hosted-link integration with the provider's inline embed if an on-page form is preferred.

The site intentionally does not collect subscriber data itself. The selected email provider should remain the system of record for consent, suppression, exports, and unsubscribes.
